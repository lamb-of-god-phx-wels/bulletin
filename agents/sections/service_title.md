# Service Title

## Format

- Title of the sermon
    - Title case (no all-caps)
    - Eras Demi ITC
    - 14 pt (17 pt leading)
    - Centered
    - Aligned to top margin
    - Border on top and bottom
        - 0.75 pt thickness
        - Flush to top margin (via \topskip=0pt)
        - Title vertically centered between borders, tight spacing
