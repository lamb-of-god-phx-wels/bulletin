\section*{WELCOME}

Thank you for joining us for worship this morning, whether in-person or online. In our service we
gather before our almighty God to offer him our worship and praise. We also gather to strengthen
ourselves through the study of God's holy and powerful Word.

\bulletinheading{Pastor's Office Hours}

If you would like to meet with Pastor Koepke, please contact him to make an appointment. Our
pastor is here to serve both Lamb of God members and our community.

\bulletinheading{Children's Room}

We love having children in our service. However, if you feel your child needs to be taken out for a
while, you may choose to use the children's room off the hallway near the mailboxes. We also ask
everyone to reserve the back few rows for families with young children, making it easier to leave
the church area if needed.
